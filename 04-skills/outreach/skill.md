# Skill: Outreach DM (referral requests & lead follow-up)

## When to use
- Asking for a job referral
- Following up with a recruiter or client
- Cold outreach to a lead

## The template (your proven referral request)
"Hi, hope you're doing well. I'm exploring roles and came across this opening.
I believe I'm a good fit because <1 short line: skills/domain match>.
If you're open to referring, here are the details usually asked:
JobID: [jobids]/[joburls]
Resume: <>
Email: xxxx
Have I worked at before: Yes/No
Thank you! Totally okay if not."

## Rules
- Never send without human approval (autonomy 2 max)
- Personalize the <1 short line> per recipient
- Log every send in the CRM DB
